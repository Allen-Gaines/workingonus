About

A place where families find strength

At Working On Us Behavioral Health & Family Services, we believe every family deserves the support, tools, and compassion needed to thrive.

Our mission

Our team of dedicated professionals walks alongside individuals and families — meeting you where you are and guiding you toward lasting change.

From behavioral health counseling to family preservation and community support, our programs are designed with one goal in mind: your well-being and the well-being of your family.

Our Story

Led by people who know this community

Working On Us was founded on the belief that behavioral health care should feel personal, not clinical. Our team lives and works in the communities we serve, across North Carolina.

Lakesha McNeil
Founder & Owner
