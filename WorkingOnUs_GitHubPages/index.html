Welcome

A place where families find strength

At Working On Us Behavioral Health & Family Services, we believe every family deserves the support, tools, and compassion needed to thrive. Our team of dedicated professionals walks alongside individuals and families — meeting you where you are and guiding you toward lasting change.

From behavioral health counseling to family preservation and community support, our programs are designed with one goal in mind: your well-being and the well-being of your family.

Getting started

What to expect

Starting care can feel like the hardest part. Here's how it works.

  1. Reach out

    Call our office or send us a message. We'll answer your questions and find a time that works for you.

  2. Your first visit

    We get to know you, talk through what brought you in, and build a plan together.

  3. Ongoing care

    Regular check-ins keep your treatment on track, with adjustments as your needs change.
