Our Services

Every family's needs look different. Whether you're here for yourself, your child, or your whole household, our programs meet you where you are right now.

💊

Medication Management

Ongoing psychiatric care that keeps your treatment plan on track and reviewed regularly. Our prescribers coordinate closely with your therapist so nothing about your care falls through the cracks.

🧭

Individual Therapy

One-on-one sessions built around your pace, your goals, your story. Our licensed clinicians work with children, teens, and adults on anxiety, depression, trauma, life transitions, and more — in person or by telehealth.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Counseling

Guided sessions that help families communicate and heal side by side. We create a steady, neutral space where every member of the family — parents, kids, and teens — has a voice.

🗂️

Case Management

A dedicated coordinator connecting you to the right resources at the right time — housing support, school advocacy, community programs, and everything in between.
