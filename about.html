About

Healing through collaboration, connection & growth

We believe healing happens through collaboration, connection, and ongoing growth.

Our mission

To provide thorough, compassionate behavioral health care that respects each client's individual path.

Working On Us is a family-centered practice in Fayetteville, North Carolina, helping individuals and families through life's hardest chapters — together. Today our focus is psychiatric evaluations and medication management, supported by therapy, family programs, and care coordination.

How we work

Three foundational pillars

Pillar I

Empowering Individuals

Helping people work through trauma, anxiety, depression, and ADHD.

  • Individual psychotherapy tailored to your story
  • Psychiatric medication management
  • Evidence-based coping skills
Pillar II

Strengthening Families

Encouraging open communication and relationships that stay steady through hard times.

  • Open, honest communication among family members
  • Direct support for caregivers, not only patients
  • Relationships that last beyond treatment
Pillar III

Supporting Successful Transitions

A safe, nonjudgmental bridge through major life changes.

  • Military discharge and reintegration into civilian life
  • Community reentry after incarceration
  • Recovery after hospitalization, divorce, and grief

Our name

What does "Working On Us" mean?

Life is a series of chapters, and navigating them shouldn't be done alone. Whether "us" means the relationship between your mind and body, you and your family, or your past and your future — we are here to help you do the work.

Our Promise

You don't have to face your next chapter alone

Wherever you are in your story, we're in it with you. Our team lives and works in the communities we serve across North Carolina.
