New Patients

New patients & questions

Everything you might want to know before your first visit — and a few things that come up along the way.

Getting started is simple

Our staff verify your insurance, walk you through intake, and schedule your psychiatric evaluation. Just call or request an appointment.

Book an AppointmentCall (910) 257-2815

What to bring

  • Photo ID and insurance card
  • A list of current medications and doses
  • Past psychiatric or hospital records, if you have them
  • A family member or caregiver, if you'd like

Insurance accepted
