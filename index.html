Welcome

What does "Working On Us" mean?

Life is a series of chapters, and navigating them shouldn't be done alone. Whether "us" means the relationship between your mind and body, you and your family, or your past and your future — we are here to help you do the work.

Our goal is to help connect who you've been with who you're becoming.

  • Empowering individuals.
  • Strengthening families.
  • Supporting successful transitions.

For referring therapists

You don't have to choose between therapy and medication management.

Patients can keep their current therapist while receiving psychiatric care here. With permission, we coordinate with the therapist — and medication management referrals don't require transferring therapy.

New Patients

Getting started is simple.

Our staff handle the details so you can focus on feeling better.

  1. Reach out

    Call (910) 257-2815 or book online. Tell us a little about what you're looking for.

  2. Insurance & intake

    We verify your insurance and walk you through intake paperwork.

  3. Your evaluation

    Meet your provider for a psychiatric evaluation and leave with a personalized plan.

What to bring

  • Photo ID and insurance card
  • A list of current medications and doses
  • Past psychiatric or hospital records, if you have them
  • A family member or caregiver, if you'd like
