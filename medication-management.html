Medication Management

Psychiatric medication management

Medication can be one helpful part of feeling more like yourself. We help you understand your options, start carefully, and adjust as you go.

What medication management means here

Medication management is ongoing psychiatric care. It begins with a psychiatric evaluation and continues with regular follow-ups, so your treatment keeps up with how you're actually doing.

We work with people experiencing trauma, anxiety, depression, ADHD, and other mental health concerns. You'll always know why a medication is being recommended, what to watch for, and what happens next.

  • In person in Fayetteville or by telehealth across North Carolina
  • Keep your current therapist — we coordinate with them, with your permission
  • Family members can be involved, if you'd like

How it works

From first visit to ongoing care

  1. Psychiatric evaluation

    Your provider reviews your symptoms, concerns, history, and relevant lab results.

  2. Your plan

    Together you discuss options — including whether medication may help — and build a personalized plan.

  3. Follow-up visits

    Regular check-ins track how well treatment is working and any side effects.

  4. Adjust as needed

    Your plan changes as your needs change, so care keeps fitting your life.

Supporting the people around you

With your permission, we help loved ones understand your treatment and how to support your progress. Our Family Medication Support program covers what to expect, side effects, warning signs, and crisis planning.

  • Photo ID and insurance card
  • A list of current medications and doses
  • Past psychiatric or hospital records, if you have them
  • A family member or caregiver, if you'd like
