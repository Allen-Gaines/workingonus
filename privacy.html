Website privacy

The practice's full website privacy notice (last updated October 15, 2026) is published at workingonus.org/privacy. In short:

  • What may be collected: information you send (like contact details and messages), and information collected automatically, such as device type, pages viewed, and cookies.
  • How it's used: to run and improve the website, respond to you, and meet legal requirements.
  • Your choices: unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in them, and manage cookies in your browser settings.
  • Children: the site isn't directed at children under 13.
  • Questions: email info@workingonus.org.

The official notice above is the one that applies. This page is a plain-language summary.

HIPAA & your health information

As a healthcare provider, the practice must protect your health information under the federal HIPAA Privacy Rule. Under HIPAA, you generally have the right to:

  • See and get a copy of your health records
  • Ask for corrections to your health information
  • Get a notice explaining how your health information may be used and shared
  • Ask that your information not be shared in certain ways
  • Ask to be contacted in a way that keeps your information private (for example, a different phone number)
  • File a complaint if you believe your rights were violated

Ask our office for a copy of the practice's Notice of Privacy Practices. You can learn more about your rights from the U.S. Department of Health & Human Services at hhs.gov/hipaa.

Please don't send health details through this website's contact form or email. Call the office instead.

Accessibility

This website was built so everyone can use it, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, or zoom. It includes:

  • A "Skip to main content" link and full keyboard navigation, with a visible focus outline
  • Meaningful page structure (one main heading per page, landmarks, labeled forms)
  • Text alternatives for images, and decorative graphics hidden from screen readers
  • Color combinations chosen for readable contrast
  • Animations that turn off when your device asks for reduced motion
  • Layouts that adjust to phones, tablets, and desktops

Having trouble using any part of the site? Call (910) 257-2815 or email info@workingonus.org and we'll help.

About the patient portal demo

The Patient Portal on this site is a student class project demo that uses made-up sample data. It is not the practice's official portal and must not be used for real health information. A real patient portal requires HIPAA-compliant hosting with a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA). Current patients should call the office for access to the official portal.
