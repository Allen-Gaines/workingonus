Specialized Programs

Specialized support for life's transitions

When medication is one part of a bigger change, these programs add therapy, family meetings, and community support.

💊 Family Medication Support 🤝 Community Reintegration 🚪 Successful Transitions 🏡 Family Restoration

Family Medication Support

Helps the whole family — not just the patient — understand treatment and how to support it.

  • What to expect from medication
  • Side effects and warning signs
  • Crisis planning
  • Caregiver roles and support

Signature program

Community Reintegration

Our signature program brings psychiatric care, therapy, family meetings, and community resources together in one place.

  • Psychiatric evaluation & medication management
  • Therapy and family meetings
  • Employment and housing referrals
  • Resource navigation and relapse prevention
Ask About This Program

Successful Transitions

A safe, nonjudgmental bridge for people facing major life changes, so no one has to face them alone.

  • Military discharge and reintegration into civilian life
  • Community reentry after incarceration
  • Recovery after hospitalization or residential treatment
  • Divorce, grief, and other big changes
Ask About This Program

Family Restoration

Improves communication, rebuilds trust, and helps families handle change together.

  • Divorce and blended families
  • Foster care reunification
  • Incarceration of a loved one
  • Mental illness or addiction recovery in the family
