Our Services

Psychiatric care and medication management come first, with therapy, family support, and care coordination built around them.

🩺

Psychiatric Evaluations

Your care starts here. Your provider reviews your symptoms, concerns, history, and relevant lab results, then works with you on a personalized treatment plan.

💊

Medication Management

We explore medication options with you and follow up regularly on how well it's working, any side effects, and your changing needs.

How medication management works →
🧭

Individual Psychotherapy

Therapy tailored to your story, with evidence-based coping skills for trauma, anxiety, depression, and ADHD.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Support

Family programs that improve communication, rebuild trust, support caregivers, and help families handle change together.

See our family programs →
🤝

Care Coordination & Support

With your permission, we collaborate with your therapist and other providers and help you find community resources.

💻

Telehealth Across North Carolina

See your provider by secure video from anywhere in North Carolina, or visit us in person in Fayetteville.

Keep your therapist

You don't have to choose between therapy and medication management. You can keep seeing your current therapist while you get psychiatric care here, and with your permission we'll coordinate with them.

Therapists: refer a patient at (910) 257-2815 →
